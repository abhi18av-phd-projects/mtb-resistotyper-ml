Two-layer resistance calls for M. tuberculosis. The curated WHO catalogue answers what it can grade; models answer the rest, and every row says which.

  1. Upload a VCF to the cluster — abc data upload <file>, or the browser upload page.
  2. Pick it below and run the models against it.
  3. Download the predictions, the reasoning, and a record of what was read.
Research use only. Not a diagnostic. Layer 2 models have not been prospectively validated and have no regulatory clearance. Do not use any output on this page to guide the treatment of a patient.
