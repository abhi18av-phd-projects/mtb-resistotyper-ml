mtb-resistotyper-ml

Upload a VCF and receive per-drug resistance predictions. Each call carries the measured operating range of the model that produced it, a reliability band, and the signed contribution of every mutation that moved it.

Layer 2 only. These models are intended to fire where a curated WHO catalogue returns Unknown or Fail. No catalogue is consulted here, so every call is a model call and none overrides a catalogue result. Research use only; not prospectively validated.
